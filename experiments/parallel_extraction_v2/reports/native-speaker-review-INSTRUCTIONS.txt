How to fill in native-speaker-review-sheet.csv
============================================

Each row is a Kapampangan sentence and its supposed Filipino translation.
Please fill in two columns:

  correct  -> y   (the Filipino is a correct translation of the Kapampangan)
              n   (it is wrong: wrong meaning, or not a translation at all)
              p   (partly right: understandable but has an error)
              ?   (you are not sure)

  comment  -> only if 'n' or 'p': say what is wrong, and if you can, write
              the correct Kapampangan and/or Filipino. Free text, any format.

Leave 'id' and 'batch' untouched (they are just for matching afterwards).

Batches, in order of importance:
  1 - main         : the sentences we most want to use. Please do these first.
  2 - extra        : lower-confidence sentences.
  3 - vocabulary   : short word / phrase pairs.
  4 - double-check : pairs already checked once; a second look.
  5 - stories      : native-authored story sentences; just a light spot-check
                     for typos / obvious slips, not a full review.

If you only have time for batch 1 (and maybe 2), that is already very useful.
