## 13. Sample Sentences & Parallel Passages (Mga Halimbawang Pangungusap)

### Narrative Snippets (Fables and Historical Narratives)

#### **Excerpt A: Bernardo Carpio**
* **Filipino:** Nang makarating sa kanilang kaalaman ang nagbabantang himagsikan, agad nilang inanyayahan si Bernardo sa isang pagpupulong. Dahil sa kanyang matatag na pamumuno at pambihirang lakas ay nabahala ang mga Kastila.
* **Kapampangan:** Inyang miras king carelang beluan ing magbantang himagsican, agad deng inanyayan I Bernardo king metung a pamipulung. Pauli ning cayang matatag a pamanimunu at pambihirang sicanan mebahala la ding Castila.
* **English:** When news of the impending revolution reached their knowledge, they immediately invited Bernardo to a meeting. Because of his firm leadership and extraordinary strength, the Spaniards became concerned.

---

#### **Excerpt B: The Turtle and the Monkey (Ang Pagong at ang Matsing)**
* **Filipino:** Ang tingin nila sa pagong ay isang kaawa-awa. Ito'y isang magandang puno, may malalaki't luntiang dahon. Ang matsing sa dalawang pabulang ito'y gumaganap ng isang papel: masakim, marahas, at matakaw.
* **Kapampangan:** Ing panakit da king pau metung yang macalunus. Iti metung yang masanting a pun, ating mangaragul a aluntiang bulung. Ing matsing caring aduang pabulang deti ginanap yang metung a papil: sakim, marahas, at matakaw.
* **English:** They view the turtle as pitiable. This is a beautiful tree with large, green leaves. The monkey in these two fables plays a role: greedy, violent, and gluttonous.

---

#### **Excerpt C: Dialogue from Iput-Ipot and Amomongo**
* **Filipino:** "Hoy, Iput-Iput, bakit lagi kang may dala-dalang ilaw?" Mekibat ya i Iput-Iput: "Dahil natatakot ako sa mga lamok." Sinabi ito ni Amomongo upang takutin ang alitaptap. Dagling lumipad si Iput-Iput sa ilong ng gorilya at inilawan niya ito.
* **Kapampangan:** "Hoy, Iput-Ipot, obat pane cang ating dala-dalang sulu?" Mekibat ya I Iput-Ipot: "Uling tatacut ku king amuc." Sinabi nang Amomongo ban tacutan ne i alitaptap. Agad yang sinulapo I Iput-Ipot king arung ning gorilya at silugian ne iti.
* **English:** "Hey, Firefly, why do you always carry a light?" Firefly replied: "Because I am afraid of mosquitoes." Amomongo said this to frighten the firefly. Firefly immediately flew to the gorilla's nose and lit it up.
